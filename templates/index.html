Pick a planning scenario
This just pre-fills the rates below with a ready-made set of assumptions - you can still tweak any of them afterwards.
1. You & Your Expenses
Used to size the retirement corpus. Default 80.
2. Income Post-Retirement
Your retirement corpus sits in an FD earning this rate; each year's interest plus your passive income is your post-retirement income, offsetting that year's expenses.
Unlike passive income above, this doesn't exist yet - it starts only in your first year of retirement, then grows every year after that at the same "Growth %/yr" rate set for passive income.
3. Medical Emergency Reserve
Treated as a recurring annual expense: it grows from your current age, and keeps growing every year through retirement, added on top of your living expenses in the corpus calculation (not a separate lump sum).
4. Current Investments & Expected Growth
Each bucket is compounded at its own rate starting from your current age, all the way to your retirement age.
Monthly SIP and Other Monthly Savings compound at their bucket's growth rate above, on top of whatever lump sum you already hold in that bucket.
5. Economic Assumptions
Defaults match the brief: 8% inflation, 10% mutual-fund growth, 5% passive-income growth, 6% FD return in retirement, life expectancy 80. All are editable.
